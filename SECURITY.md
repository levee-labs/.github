# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected security vulnerabilities privately to
[security@levee.io](mailto:security@levee.io). Please include enough detail to
reproduce and assess the issue without including data that belongs to another
person or organization.

Levee will acknowledge the report within 3 business days and provide a status
update within 10 business days.

## Safe harbour

Levee supports good-faith security research that does not access other
people's or organizations' data, degrade service, or exfiltrate any data. We
will not initiate legal action against researchers who follow those boundaries
and make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations and operational harm.
